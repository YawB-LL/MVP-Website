Accordion FAQ
What is a token?
A digital certificate representing your ownership share in a specific property-holding SPV.


What does fractional ownership mean?
You’re co-investing with others in a property. You earn based on your proportion of ownership.


Is this cryptocurrency?
No. LandLedger does not use crypto. We use blockchain only for ownership tracking—not coins or speculation.


What is blockchain and why is it used?
Blockchain is a tamper-proof digital ledger that ensures your ownership is secure, transparent, and verifiable.


Do I need a crypto wallet?
No. You get a custodial wallet on the platform—secure and easy to use.


What is a custodial wallet? Is it safe?
Yes. LandLedger safely stores your tokens for you—protected with bank-grade security.


What is an SPV?
A Special Purpose Vehicle is a legally registered company that owns the property. You own part of the SPV, not LandLedger itself.


Can I sell my tokens?
Yes - after the holding period. You can sell through a resale window to vetted users, with a Right of First Refusal (ROFR) offered to existing investors.
Ownership per investor is capped at 30%, even through resale.


Will there be a secondary market?
Yes. LandLedger is developing a regulated secondary marketplace to enable broader token liquidity over time.


What if LandLedger shuts down?
Your investment is safe. You own part of the SPV that owns the property, not the platform.


Is this regulated?
We are structured to comply with VASP legislation and are in dialogue with Ghana’s key regulators.


What is KYC/AML and why does it matter?
KYC = Know Your Customer
AML = Anti-Money Laundering
These safeguards protect both you and the platform from fraud and financial crime.
